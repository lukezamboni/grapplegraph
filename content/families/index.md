Use these families to compare related requirements across belts. A technique appears under its primary study family; its sequence links reveal the shared positions, movements, and submissions.

- [[families/self-defence|Self-defence]] — Standing responses, releases, and distance-management sequences.
- [[families/half-guard|Half guard]] — Half-, deep-half-, and knee-shield sequences from top and bottom.
- [[families/guard-passing|Guard passing]] — Passing sequences and pass reactions that lead to control or a finish.
- [[families/sweeps|Sweeps]] — Reversals that create top position, mount, or back exposure.
- [[families/takedowns|Takedowns]] — Standing entries that establish top control or connect to a finish.
- [[families/guard-attacks-and-transitions|Guard attacks and transitions]] — Submissions, back takes, and positional chains initiated from guard or another control position.

See also [[families/related-techniques|Related techniques]] for cross-family drilling groups and shared finishes.
