# Curated pathways

System maps are deliberately arranged study aids. Unlike the automatic graph, each branch communicates a specific positional decision or curriculum relationship.

## [[maps/Closed guard system.canvas|Closed guard system]]

Read common reactions from closed guard and connect them to five Blue-belt exam sequences: a sweep, linked arm attacks, a Kimura, and two arm-drag outcomes.

## [[maps/De La Riva system.canvas|De La Riva system]]

Compare forward, backward, back-take, and lapel-assisted sweep directions across the Blue- and Purple-belt syllabi.

## [[maps/Standing pathways.canvas|Standing pathways]]

Separate self-defence reactions from grappling entries, then connect the standing curriculum to takedowns, control positions, and finishes.

> [!note] Academy version comes first
> These maps explain relationships between requirements. They do not replace the exact grips, timing, or decision rules taught by your Renzo Gracie Niagara instructor.

## Map or graph?

- Use a **system map** when you want a curated pathway with meaningful branch labels.
- Use the **interactive graph** when you want to discover every connection created by shared positions, movements, and submissions.
- Use the [[exams/Exam catalogue.base|exam catalogue]] when you want a structured list or comparison.
