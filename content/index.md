## See the whole sequence

GrappleGraph turns a 30-technique Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu exam into a connected study atlas. Follow a sequence from its **starting position**, through its **primary movement**, to its **landing position** and **finish**—then use the graph to discover what it shares with the rest of the exam.

> [!note] Academy version comes first
> These notes are study aids. Grips, details, and sequence order should be verified with your instructor before the exam.
