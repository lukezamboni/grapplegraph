# Choose a demo

These are intentionally small proofs of concept. They use the same GrappleGraph notes, links, and Renzo Gracie Niagara visual language as the rest of the atlas.

## [[demos/exam-catalogue.base|Exam catalogue · Bases]]

A structured catalogue generated from technique frontmatter. Switch between all belts, belt-specific tables, and a compact list. Sort a table by clicking a column heading.

**Best for:** checking requirements, comparing metadata, finding incomplete records, and scanning one belt at a time.

## [[demos/closed-guard-attack-tree.canvas|Closed-guard attack tree · Canvas]]

A hand-arranged decision tree showing how common reactions connect to five blue-belt exam sequences. Pan, zoom, and open a linked technique node.

**Best for:** game plans, decision trees, positional systems, and showing why techniques connect.

> [!note] What this comparison demonstrates
> Bases derives its layout from structured data. Canvas preserves a layout that an instructor or student deliberately designed. The existing GrappleGraph graph remains the automatic, exploratory view.
